## 2025–2026 Market Shift (investor lens)

- **Mega-rounds moved to non-traditional leads.** Trading firms (Jane Street, Jump), new crossover vehicles (Trace Capital, Innovation X, Situational Awareness), asset managers (Neuberger Berman) and sovereign/national funds (QIA, Korea National Growth Fund) led or anchored most $200M+ AI-chip rounds in 2026, while classic early-stage VCs (Sutter Hill, Playground, Lux, DCVC) were largely absent from the headline leads [TO VERIFY — pattern drawn from rounds below].
- **Exits arrived — but mostly not as classic M&A.** Groq's ~$20B non-exclusive licensing + leadership hire by NVIDIA (Dec 2025) [CONFIRMED], Marvell's close of Celestial AI (2 Feb 2026) [CONFIRMED], and Cerebras' Nasdaq IPO (14 May 2026, ~$5.55B raised at $185/share) [CONFIRMED] returned capital to Disruptive, BlackRock, Maverick Silicon, Fidelity, Atreides, Benchmark, Eclipse and others. Licence-plus-acqui-hire structures mean preference stacks and payout mechanics (Groq: reported ~85% upfront, ~10% mid-2026, remainder end-2026, per Axios [TO VERIFY]) must be diligenced.
- **Inference silicon is the dominant thesis.** Step-ups of 2–4x within 6–12 months (Etched $5B → $10.3B → $21B; FuriosaAI ~$735M → $2.9B; Tenstorrent ~$2.6B → $6.2B) [TO VERIFY individual figures as tagged below] reflect the inference shift; diligence valuation risk accordingly.
- **New dedicated semiconductor/deep-tech vehicles:** Maverick Silicon (Form D: ~$812.7M sold, first sale Jan 2025) [TO VERIFY]; Eclipse Fund VI ($720M) + Early Growth III ($591M), Apr 2026 [CONFIRMED]; Cloudberry VC €30M European semiconductor fund (May 2026, GlobalFoundries among strategic LPs) [TO VERIFY]; Transition Ventures $150M AI/robotics/power fund (May 2026) [TO VERIFY]; Deep33 Ventures $150M target, $100M first close (Jan 2026) [TO VERIFY]; Celesta Capital planned ₹2,000 Cr India deep-tech fund (Mar 2026) [UNCONFIRMED].

## Co-Investment / Syndicate Signals
- **Sutter Hill + crossover (Fidelity/Atreides)** → connectivity/fabric path to IPO (Astera precedent).
- **Strategic (NVIDIA/Samsung/Intel) + top VC** → strong validation *and* exit signal; watch these syndicates closely. 2026 example: Ayar Labs E combined Neuberger Berman/Sequoia with AMD, NVIDIA, MediaTek and Alchip [CONFIRMED].
- **Temasek/BlackRock presence** → late-stage, large-check, patient — signals category maturation.
- **Trading firms (Jane Street, Jump) + AGI-thesis funds (Situational Awareness)** → 2026 signature syndicate for transformer-specific inference ASICs (MatX, Etched, Positron).
- **Sovereign/national funds (QIA, Korea NGF, Temasek) + domestic strategics** → national-champion inference silicon (Rebellions, FuriosaAI, d-Matrix); IPO path rather than M&A.
- **Licence-plus-acquihire exits (NVIDIA–Groq, NVIDIA–Enfabrica [TO VERIFY])** → model investor returns on licence payouts, not equity sales; check antitrust-driven structures.
