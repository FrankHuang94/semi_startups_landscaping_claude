## Co-Investment / Syndicate Signals
- **Sutter Hill + crossover (Fidelity/Atreides)** → connectivity/fabric path to IPO (Astera precedent).
- **Strategic (NVIDIA/Samsung/Intel) + top VC** → strong validation *and* exit signal; watch these syndicates closely.
- **Temasek/BlackRock presence** → late-stage, large-check, patient — signals category maturation.
- **(2026) Quant/prop firm co-leading a Series B** → the company has a measurable, benchmarkable performance claim and a buyer of compute in the syndicate. Fast to close, price-insensitive at the top end, and less helpful on GTM than a classic silicon VC — check who is actually doing the customer work.
- **(2026) Sovereign fund + domestic strategic** (Korea NGF + KT; UK sovereign AI fund + Arm) → policy-driven capital with an IPO-on-home-exchange endpoint. Underwrite the local listing path, not a US strategic exit.
- **(2026) Existing investors re-upping without a new lead** (Lightmatter, several Korean pre-IPO rounds) → flat-to-soft pricing; look for structure.
