## Funding Environment Notes
- **Scale reset (2026):** rounds that were "mega" in 2024 are mid-sized now. Semiconductor startups drew roughly **$10.7B** from seed to pre-IPO in 2026 YTD, on pace to exceed 2025 [TO VERIFY]; one tracker counts **61 disclosed semi deals / ~$7.1B** for July 2025–June 2026 [TO VERIFY].
- **Concentration:** Mega-rounds cluster in inference (Etched, SambaNova, Positron), optical (Ayar, OLIX), training (MatX), and quantum; seed/A is broad across AI-EDA, CXL, and chiplets.
- **Crossover presence** (Fidelity, BlackRock, T. Rowe, Capital Group, General Atlantic) at growth stage signals pre-IPO positioning — vindicated by the Cerebras IPO (May 2026).
- **Prop-trading and quant capital is now a first-tier source of silicon capital:** Jane Street (MatX, Etched), Jump Trading (Positron), Hudson River Trading (OLIX), Situational Awareness LP (MatX). New behavior vs. the 2024 syndicate mix.
- **Strategic co-investment** (NVIDIA, Arm, Samsung, Micron, MediaTek, AMD, Meta, Marvell, SK hynix, Cisco, Lumentum) anchors an increasing share of rounds — validation *and* a pre-signal of the acquirer set.
- **Sovereign capital is explicit:** Korea's National Growth Fund (Rebellions), the UK sovereign AI venture fund (OLIX), EuroHPC DARE grants (Axelera), QIA (Positron), MGX (~$49B Fund I).
- **Geography:** Korea (Rebellions, FuriosaAI, DeepX, Panmnesia) is now an IPO story rather than a venture story; Europe produced its largest-ever semi VC round (OLIX, $312M).
- **Valuation dispersion is extreme:** Etched doubled to ~$10.3B in ~7 months while Hailo's valuation fell by more than half before a distressed sale — same category, opposite direction.
