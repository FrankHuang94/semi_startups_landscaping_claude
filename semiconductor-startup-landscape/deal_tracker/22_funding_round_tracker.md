## Funding Environment Notes
- **Concentration:** Mega-rounds cluster in inference (Groq), optical (Lightmatter/Celestial), and quantum (PsiQuantum); seed/A is broad across EDA, CXL, and chiplets.
- **Crossover presence** (Fidelity, BlackRock, T. Rowe) at growth stage signals pre-IPO positioning in optical + inference.
- **Strategic co-investment** (NVIDIA, Samsung, Intel Capital, Micron) increasingly anchors rounds — validation + exit signal.
- **Geography:** strong Korea (Rebellions, FuriosaAI, DeepX, Panmnesia) and EU (Axelera, Codasip) sovereign-tailwind activity.
