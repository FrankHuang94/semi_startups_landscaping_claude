## Funding Environment Notes
- **Mega-round era (2025H2–2026):** Inference silicon dominates: Groq $750M (Sep-25), Cerebras $1.1B (Sep-25) + $1B (Feb-26) then a $5.55B IPO (May-26), Etched ~$500M (Jan-26) → reported $700M at ~$21B (Aug-26), MatX $500M, Positron $230M, d-Matrix $275M, FuriosaAI $620M, Tenstorrent $1.5B (Oct-26). Valuations step up 2–4× within 6–12 months for companies with a named anchor customer (Jane Street for Etched, Jump Trading for Positron).
- **Quarterly aggregates (Semiconductor Engineering startup-funding series, via secondary sources):** Q1 2026 ≈ **$8.4B across ~80 startups, 18 rounds >$100M**, with Rapidus and Cerebras each >$1B [TO VERIFY]; Q3 2026 "AI dominated funding … two inference companies each announcing $1B" (companies not named in snippet) [TO VERIFY]. A conflicting secondary figure (~$2.0B / 75 startups for a quarter labeled Q1 2026) likely reflects a different scope [UNCONFIRMED]. Q2 2026 report not located. YTD to June 2026 ≈ $10.7B per one aggregator [UNCONFIRMED].
- **Regional:** Europe semiconductor startups €1.6B across 23 rounds in H1 2026 (+12.2% vs H1 2025) per funding.tech.eu [TO VERIFY]; India semiconductor startups ~$61.9M in H1 2026 [TO VERIFY]. Korea: sovereign capital now anchors rounds directly (National Growth Fund $280M into FuriosaAI).
- **New capital types:** Quant/HFT firms (Jane Street, Jump Trading) act as lead investor *and* first customer; sovereign funds (QIA in d-Matrix, Ayar Labs, Positron; Temasek; EDBI) are near-ubiquitous at growth stage; crypto treasuries appear (Tether in Tenstorrent [TO VERIFY]).
- **AI-for-chip-design boom:** Ricursive ($300M A at $4B, ~2 months after launch), ChipAgents ($134M Series A total), Cognichip ($60M A) — the EDA category is seeing software-style valuations.
- **Interconnect/optical:** Upscale AI ($390M across A/A-1 in 2026, NVIDIA in A-1), Ayar Labs ($650M in 2026 incl. extension [TO VERIFY ext.]), plus Celestial AI's ~$3.25B exit to Marvell set the comp for CPO/scale-up fabrics.
- **Strategic co-investment** (NVIDIA, AMD, Arm, Intel, Marvell, Micron, MediaTek, Samsung) increasingly anchors rounds — validation + exit signal; NVIDIA now both invests in and licenses from would-be competitors.
- **Geography:** strong Korea (Rebellions, FuriosaAI, DeepX, Panmnesia, Xcena) and EU (Axelera, Codasip) sovereign-tailwind activity.
