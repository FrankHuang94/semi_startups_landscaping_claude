AMD agreed to invest up to $5B in Anthropic tied to up to 2 GW of AMD chips from 1H 2027 (Jul 2026) [CONFIRMED per Reuters/Zawya].

## Selected Strategic Profiles

### NVIDIA (NVentures + direct investments)
- **Motivation:** strengthen the AI ecosystem, pre-empt architectural threats, and lock in demand; particular interest in networking/fabric and optical interconnect. **CVC portfolio/bets:** Ayar Labs (Series E, Mar 2026) [CONFIRMED], Xscape Photonics, Enfabrica (pre-deal), plus broad AI-infra; NVentures reportedly did ~30 deals in 2025 and ~20 in Jan–May 2026 [UNCONFIRMED — tracker data]. **Direct mega-investments:** Intel $5B (closed 26 Dec 2025, ~4%; no special governance rights) [CONFIRMED]; Synopsys $2B (Dec 2025) [CONFIRMED]; Nokia ~$1B (Oct 2025) [TO VERIFY value]; Anthropic up to $10B (Nov 2025) [CONFIRMED]; OpenAI ~$30B (2026, at ~$730B pre-money per reports) [TO VERIFY]. Aggregate 2026 AI equity commitments reported at >$40B [UNCONFIRMED]. **Acquisition history:** Mellanox; Groq ~$20B non-exclusive licence + leadership hire (Dec 2025) [CONFIRMED]; Enfabrica team + licence (~$900M, Sep 2025) [TO VERIFY]. **VC relevance:** an NVIDIA check remains the strongest validation signal and flags a likely customer and potential "acquirer" — but exits increasingly come as licence + acquihire, which can leave a residual company (GroqCloud) and staged payouts. Critics flag circular vendor-financing risk. **Last updated:** 2026-11-04.

### Samsung Catalyst Fund
- **Motivation:** adjacency to memory (HBM) and foundry; secure leading-edge AI-silicon partners/customers. **Portfolio:** Tenstorrent (led Series D), Groq (licensed to NVIDIA), Blaize, Axelera, Eliyan (Series C, Jan 2026) [TO VERIFY], Normal Computing (led $50M, Mar 2026) [TO VERIFY], Rebellions (pre-IPO, Mar 2026) [CONFIRMED]. **Exits:** Teramount (to Molex, Apr 2026) and Empower (to ADI, 2026) reported [TO VERIFY]. **VC relevance:** strong APAC validation; potential foundry/memory partner and acquirer. **Last updated:** 2026-11-04.

### Intel Capital
- **Status correction:** Intel announced a spin-out in Jan 2025 but **reversed it in Apr 2025** (CEO Lip-Bu Tan, Q1 2025 call); Intel Capital stays in-house, focused on monetising the portfolio and more selective new investments [CONFIRMED — TechCrunch, Fortune]. Earlier DB versions describing it as "now independent" were wrong. **Portfolio:** Rivos (acquired by Meta, 2025 [TO VERIFY]), Eliyan, Empower (reported exit [TO VERIFY]), Movellus, MemVerge, Quantum Machines, SambaNova. **Corporate context:** NVIDIA's $5B stake closed Dec 2025 [CONFIRMED]; Intel–SambaNova acquisition talks (~$1.6B incl. debt) reportedly stalled in Jan 2026 [UNCONFIRMED]; Intel and Qualcomm reportedly explored Tenstorrent before its Oct 2026 round [UNCONFIRMED]. **VC relevance:** fewer new lead cheques; expect secondary sales of holdings. **Last updated:** 2026-11-04.

### AMD (AMD Ventures + corporate)
- **Motivation:** build a full-stack alternative to NVIDIA and secure Instinct demand. **2026 activity:** co-led TensorWave $350M B at $1.55B with Magnetar (Jun 2026) [CONFIRMED]; Ayar Labs E (Mar 2026) [CONFIRMED]; up to $5B Anthropic investment linked to up to 2 GW of AMD chips (Jul 2026) [CONFIRMED]. **Acquisitions:** ZT Systems, Silo AI, Untether AI team (2025) [TO VERIFY]. **VC relevance:** AMD increasingly funds its own demand (AMD-only clouds, model labs); for chip startups AMD is a co-investor/acquirer of teams and IP more than of whole companies. **Last updated:** 2026-11-04.

### Qualcomm (Qualcomm Ventures + corporate)

## How to Use This for Syndication & Exit
1. **Validation:** a relevant strategic on the cap table de-risks technical and demand uncertainty.
2. **Exit mapping:** match each portfolio company to its 2–3 most logical strategic acquirers early — and model licence-plus-acquihire outcomes (NVIDIA–Groq) alongside classic M&A.
3. **Roadmap signal:** strategics invest where their roadmap has gaps — their bets telegraph the next category (2026: optical I/O/CPO, scale-up fabrics, inference ASICs).
4. **Caution:** strategic terms (ROFR, info rights) can complicate later rounds/exits; diligence them. Also diligence circular deals where the investor is also the supplier or customer (vendor financing).
