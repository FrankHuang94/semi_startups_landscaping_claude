## Deal Notes (selected)

### NVIDIA → Groq (license + acqui-hire, Dec 2025) and NVIDIA → Enfabrica (Sept 2025)
- **Background:** Rather than buying companies outright (and inviting antitrust review), NVIDIA paid for **non-exclusive IP licenses plus leadership/engineering hires**. Enfabrica (~$900M+, per CNBC) brought CEO Rochan Sankar and the ACF/EMFASYS memory-fabric NIC tech; Groq (~$20B reported by CNBC, undisclosed officially) brought TPU co-inventor Jonathan Ross, president Sunny Madra and core LPU engineers. Groq remains a standalone company running GroqCloud under new CEO Simon Edwards. **Rationale:** absorb the most credible inference-specific architecture (deterministic SRAM-heavy LPU) and scale-out memory fabric before rivals do. **Implied category value:** inference silicon with proven software + cloud revenue can command ~3× last private mark; "keep the fiction of competition alive" (analyst quoted by CNBC) — i.e., remaining shell has residual value but lost its founders. **Investor outcome:** how license proceeds reach Groq/Enfabrica shareholders has not been publicly disclosed [TO VERIFY]. **Lesson for VCs:** underwrite the reverse-acquihire as a real exit path; diligence protective provisions on how license proceeds are distributed and what happens to employees who do not move.

### Marvell → Celestial AI (announced Dec 2025, closed Feb 2026)
- **Background:** Largest pure-play startup acquisition in optical interconnect to date. **Rationale:** own optical scale-up (Photonic Fabric) as copper reach runs out at rack scale; complements Marvell's custom-XPU and electro-optics business. **Implied category value:** validates [06](../categories/06_optical_interconnect_and_cpo.md) CPO/optical-I/O as a multi-billion strategic category; peers (Ayar Labs, Lightmatter) now benchmark against it. **Lesson:** optical I/O startups' natural acquirers are custom-silicon houses (Marvell, Broadcom) and GPU vendors.

### Microchip → Hailo (Jul–Sep 2026) and NXP → Kinara (2025)
- **Background:** Two edge-AI NPU startups sold to MCU/automotive incumbents at modest values (Kinara $307M; Hailo undisclosed, "not material"). **Implied category value:** [03](../categories/03_edge_inference_chips.md) exits cluster at sub-$500M strategic tuck-ins; standalone scale is hard against NXP/Qualcomm/Microchip integrated SoCs. **Lesson:** price edge-AI rounds against a $200–500M strategic exit unless there is a clear path to $100M+ revenue.

### Meta → Rivos (2025) and Intel ↔ SambaNova (2025–26)
- **Background:** Hyperscalers buy custom-silicon teams (Meta–Rivos for MTIA); incumbents shopping for AI stacks can walk (Intel–SambaNova talks stalled; Intel took a minority stake in a $350M Series E instead). **Lesson:** a hyperscaler customer relationship is the most reliable precursor to acquisition; legacy-valuation overhang (SambaNova's ~$5B 2021 mark) can block deals.

### Startup-to-startup consolidation (2026)
- d-Matrix bought GigaIO's data-center business (rack fabric) and Unconventional AI agreed to buy NeuReality (team + Israel site). **Signal:** well-funded 2025–26 entrants are acquiring sub-scale peers for systems capability and talent — a new floor exit for older Series B/C inference startups.

### SoftBank → Graphcore (2024)
- **Background:** Graphcore (UK IPU pioneer) struggled commercially against NVIDIA; acquired by SoftBank reportedly well below its ~$2.8B peak valuation. **Rationale:** assemble an AI-compute portfolio around Arm (later extended with Ampere, closed Nov 2025). **Technology acquired:** IPU dataflow architecture, UK silicon talent. **Implied category value:** training-chip startups face brutal incumbent economics; "great tech, no ecosystem" is a recurring failure mode. **Investor outcome:** likely a loss/down outcome for late investors. **Lesson for VCs:** in training silicon, software ecosystem + demand anchor matter more than architecture elegance.

### Infineon → GaN Systems (2023)
- **Background:** Infineon acquired GaN Systems to consolidate GaN power leadership. **Rationale:** datacenter/EV power demand for GaN. **Implied category value:** validates [08](../categories/08_power_semiconductors_and_power_delivery.md) — power-semi startups have clean strategic exits to Infineon/Renesas/ADI. **Lesson:** power startups should underwrite to strategic M&A multiples.

### AMD → ZT Systems + Silo AI + Enosemi + Brium + Untether team (2024–25)
- **Background:** AMD assembled systems (ZT), software (Silo AI, Brium), photonics (Enosemi) and inference talent (Untether team hire — not an acquisition of the company) to close the gap with NVIDIA's full-stack. **Implied category value:** acquirers want **systems + software + IP**, not just chips; talent acqui-hires are a real (if modest) exit for inference startups that miss escape velocity. **Lesson:** position for the full-stack acquirer thesis.
