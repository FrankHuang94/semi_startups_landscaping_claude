# 10 — RF, Wireless & Connectivity

> Lowest VC priority for AI datacenter; mostly incumbent-dominated (Qorvo/Skyworks/Broadcom/Qualcomm). Tracked for completeness and selective edge/IoT, 5G/AI-RAN, SATCOM and mmWave interest. 2025–26: Morse Micro and Movandi raised; Pharrowtech failed — mmWave FWA demand remains thin.

## 1. VC Investment Thesis
- **Why now (limited):** RF front-ends and low-power connectivity matter for edge/IoT and physical-AI devices; AI-RAN (GPU/accelerator-based baseband) is reopening base-station silicon; SATCOM phased arrays and mmWave see niche innovation.
- **Venture-scale:** Low for AI-DC; selective for software-defined 5G baseband (EdgeQ), IoT connectivity standards plays (Morse Micro/Wi-Fi HaLow), SATCOM/defense phased arrays.
- **Acquirers:** Qualcomm, Qorvo, Skyworks, Broadcom, MACOM, MediaTek, Nokia/Ericsson (RAN), Renesas. **Exit:** IP/acqui-hire mostly; occasional strategic.
- **Winning startup:** standards-led connectivity silicon with a volume anchor (HaLow), or programmable 5G/AI-RAN silicon with operator traction. **Non-investable:** commodity RF FE vs. incumbents; mmWave FWA without carrier pull (see Pharrowtech).

## 2. Market Context
- **Structure:** RF incumbents (Qualcomm, Qorvo, Skyworks, Broadcom, MACOM) dominate; thin startup layer across baseband, mmWave, IoT Wi-Fi.
- **Segments:** Handset/IoT FE, Wi-Fi HaLow/IoT, 5G small cells & AI-RAN, SATCOM/defense phased arrays, mmWave repeaters/backhaul, automotive radar.
- **Drivers:** 5G-Advanced/6G, AI-RAN programs (NVIDIA-led ecosystem) [TO VERIFY], LEO satellite, defense, IoT/physical-AI connectivity.
- **2025–26 events:** Morse Micro US$59M Series C led by MegaChips (Sept 2025) [CONFIRMED]; Movandi $40M (Oct 2025) [CONFIRMED]; Pharrowtech (imec mmWave spinout) went bankrupt (~May 2025, per Bits&Chips) [CONFIRMED]. No new EdgeQ or Pivotal Commware round found [TO VERIFY].
- **Bottlenecks:** Linearity/efficiency, integration, III-V cost, carrier capex cycles. **Risks:** incumbent scale, low AI-DC pull, mmWave demand.
