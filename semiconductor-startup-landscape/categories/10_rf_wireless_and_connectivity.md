# 10 — RF, Wireless & Connectivity

> Lowest VC priority for AI datacenter; mostly incumbent-dominated (Qorvo/Skyworks/Broadcom). Tracked for completeness and selective edge/SATCOM/mmWave interest. Mostly acqui-hire/IP exits.

## 1. VC Investment Thesis
- **Why now (limited):** RF front-ends matter for edge/IoT AI and SATCOM phased arrays; mmWave and III-V integration see niche innovation.
- **Venture-scale:** Low for AI-DC; selective for SATCOM/defense phased arrays and mmWave.
- **Acquirers:** Qorvo, Skyworks, Broadcom, Qualcomm, MACOM. **Exit:** IP/acqui-hire mostly.
- **Winning startup:** differentiated mmWave/phased-array IP with a defense/SATCOM anchor. **Non-investable:** commodity RF FE vs. incumbents.

## 2. Market Context
- **Structure:** RF incumbents (Qorvo, Skyworks, Broadcom, MACOM, Qualcomm) dominate; thin startup layer.
- **Segments:** Handset/IoT FE, SATCOM/defense phased arrays, mmWave backhaul, automotive radar.
- **Drivers:** 5G/6G, LEO satellite, defense, edge connectivity.
- **Bottlenecks:** Linearity/efficiency, integration, III-V cost. **Risks:** incumbent scale, low AI-DC pull.
