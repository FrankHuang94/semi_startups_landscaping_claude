# Corporate Research Labs Index

- **Areas:** Accelerator architecture, interconnect, ML-for-EDA (ChipNeMo), efficient inference, photonics
- **Why It Matters:** Sets the frontier and the bar; alumni found companies across [02](../categories/02_inference_accelerators.md)/[04](../categories/04_custom_asic_and_chiplets.md)/[12](../categories/12_eda_ip_and_design_tools.md). **Key people (public):** Bill Dally, Brucek Khailany. **Startup Formation Potential:** High (via departures) · **VC Relevance:** 5 · **Confidence:** High · **Last updated:** 2026-06-09
