## Scoring
- **Signal Strength:** Very High / High / Medium / Low (per the signal framework)
- **VC Relevance:** 1–5
- **Outreach Priority** = function of (signal strength × VC relevance × bottleneck severity)
- **Status:** Confirmed / Likely / Weak / Watchlist Only
