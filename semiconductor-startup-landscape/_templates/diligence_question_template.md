# Diligence Question Bank — [Category / Company]

## Technical Diligence
- What is the core architectural bet, and what physics/economics makes it durable?
- What process node, IP, and EDA dependencies exist? Tape-out status and yield?
- What are the independently verifiable performance/efficiency benchmarks (perf/W, perf/$, latency)?
- What is the software stack maturity (compiler, kernels, framework support)?
- What breaks at scale (thermal, power delivery, packaging, interconnect)?

## Market Diligence
- What is the real, near-term serviceable market vs. the narrative TAM?
- Who is the budget owner, and is this a line-item or a science project?
- How fast is the inflection (workload, model architecture, datacenter spend)?

## Customer Diligence
- Which design wins / pilots are contractually committed vs. LOIs?
- What is customer concentration and switching cost?
- What is the sales cycle and qualification timeline for hyperscalers/OEMs?

## Competitive Diligence
- How does this survive an NVIDIA/AMD/Broadcom roadmap response?
- What is defensible: IP, ecosystem lock-in, manufacturing access, talent?
- What substitutes (software, existing silicon) erode the wedge?

## Financial Diligence
- Burn, runway, capital required to first revenue and to scale?
- Gross margin trajectory and BOM/packaging cost structure?
- Funding-round risk and strategic-investor dependence?

## Founder Diligence
- Founder–market fit; prior silicon shipped; ability to recruit scarce talent?
- Cap table health, option pool, prior investor reputations?

## Exit Diligence
- Realistic acquirers and strategic rationale; precedent multiples?
- IPO viability given capital intensity and revenue scale?
- What outcome returns the fund, and what is the probability?
